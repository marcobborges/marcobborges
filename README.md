**Product-minded engineer working at the intersection of Data Infrastructure, Reliability, and Developer Experience.**

---

### What I've been up to

Over the past two years working in a highly technical platform team, I've had the opportunity to lead a few initiatives I'm particularly proud of:

**Reducing critical incidents across the org**
I led an effort to significantly reduce Sev-1 and Sev-2 incidents that were impacting machine learning models — and indirectly, customers. This involved rethinking monitoring, improving runbooks, and driving accountability across teams. The result: a meaningfully better reliability posture for the entire data platform.

**Cutting support ticket volume through transparency**
I worked on reducing the support burden on the Data org by improving documentation quality, increasing transparency around common issues, and integrating AI-powered self-service tools (think ChatGPT-based internal assistants) so that users could find answers from existing docs before opening a ticket.

**AI agent for program engagement tracking**
I built a lightweight AI agent to automate the collection of engagement metrics for a cross-team program. What used to take roughly a week of manual work now runs in about two hours — pulling data, computing participation rates, and surfacing insights automatically.

**Small automations, big impact**
Working in a team with significant tech debt and KTLO overhead, I found opportunities to lead small but meaningful automations on my own — using tools like [Cursor](https://cursor.com) and [Claude](https://claude.ai) to prototype and ship quickly, without waiting for a full engineering cycle.

### How I think about work

I believe the best product work in platform teams comes from **deeply understanding the pain** of your internal users, then finding the shortest path to relief — whether that's a policy change, a doc rewrite, or a quick automation. Not everything needs to be a quarter-long project.
